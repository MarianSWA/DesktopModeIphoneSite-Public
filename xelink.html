Show XeOS in this browser

  1. In XeOS, open Screen Sharing.
  2. Click Mirror XeOS on Another Device and turn on Mirror XeOS.
  3. Share or copy the link, then open it in any browser on the same network.

Then type the address XeOS shows, or open the link it shared.
