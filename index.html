Windows and multitasking

Open apps side by side on the external display, resize them and switch between them from the dock, the way you would on a computer.

Fullscreen YouTube, Netflix and all streaming apps

YouTube and other video sites play full screen at the display's own resolution. No black bars, no letterboxing, on a monitor, TV or projector.

Mouse and keyboard

Point and click your way: a Bluetooth mouse, your iPhone's screen as a trackpad, or the iPhone itself as an Air Mouse you move through the air. Typing works the same way, with a physical keyboard and shortcuts, the on-screen keyboard, or dictation.

Developer apps

A full SSH Terminal with saved servers, keys kept in the iPhone's Keychain and a file browser for the remote machine. Pair it with VS Code Remote Tunnels in the browser and your iPhone becomes a real dev workstation.

TV, smart glasses, projector, car display

Anything with an HDMI or USB-C input becomes a desktop: a hotel TV, AR smart glasses, a projector in a meeting room or the screen in your car. Pick the resolution, frame rate, scale and rotation that suit it.

Cloud gaming on your TV

Play Xbox Cloud Gaming, GeForce NOW and Amazon Luna on the big screen with a game controller.

Two screens on iPhone Duo and iPad

Use the iPhone Duo's inner screen or your iPad as a second display next to your monitor, and move windows between them.

Files, iCloud and USB drives

Browse iCloud Drive, files on your iPhone and external SSDs in a desktop file manager, with ZIP archives and a Trash.

Word, Excel and PowerPoint

Preview Office documents at full size on the external screen, zoom in and copy cells from spreadsheets.

PDF editor

Read, annotate and present PDFs, fill in forms and add your signature.

Photos and slideshows

Your photo library, albums and videos on the big screen, with full-screen slideshows.

Apple integration

Your Apple Calendar, Reminders and Photos, synced from your Apple account, on the big screen. Plan the week in a full month view, tick off reminders and play your albums as a slideshow on the TV.

Websites as apps

Install any site to the dock and open it in its own window, like a desktop app. It can even open your files.

Smart Search

Press Control + Space to find apps, files, events and clipboard history, calculate and convert currencies.

Built-in ad blocker

Fewer ads in the browser, including many video ads, with a switch per site when you need it.

In-app support

You can have peace of mind: we constantly hear your feedback, improve the app with every update and always help you through easy-to-use support right inside XeOS. Most questions get an answer within a day.
